Scenario: no recovery action

The HV switchgear failed its factory test and arrived 3 weeks late. On its own that sounds small. Paramap traced it through the plan: one week was absorbed by float, but the electrical crew was released to another site while they waited, and the pushed-out commissioning now lands in the two-week Christmas shutdown. Those two effects turned a 3-week supplier delay into a 5-week handover slip.

Reasoning, in plain language

1. Supplier delay, +3 weeks. Switchgear was due 21 Sep 2026. It failed its factory acceptance test and was re-tested, arriving 12 Oct 2026.
2. Float, −1 week. Energising the main board had 1 week of free float, so only 2 weeks reached the next task.
3. Crew remobilisation, +1 week. The electrical subcontractor moved 40 of 52 electricians to another job during the wait. Bringing them back and re-inducting them costs a week before Zone B install can resume at full rate.
4. Calendar, +2 weeks. Commissioning was planned to finish before the site shutdown (21 Dec 2026 to 1 Jan 2027). Moved 3 weeks later, it now spans the shutdown and stops for 2 weeks.

Paramap found steps 3 and 4 automatically: step 3 from the resource assignments in the plan, step 4 from the project calendar. Neither is visible if you only look at the late task.

Q6 · What should management do?

Recovery options

Pick any combination. Every number on this page, including the headline and the charts above, recalculates for the options you choose.

Compare scenarios

Recommendation

Approve options C and D now, claim against the switchgear supplier, and re-baseline the tenant's handover expectation to the new P80 date.

  1. C and D together recover 3 of the 5 weeks for about $230k, and avoid around $1.1M of combined cost and lost rent compared with doing nothing.
  2. Adding weekend shifts (B) recovers one more week but stacks fatigue risk on top of the holiday crew, during the testing phase where defects are hardest to find. The extra $140k saving is not worth that risk.
  3. The supply contract allows delay damages of up to $210k. That money is not counted above, so treat it as upside.
  4. Decide by 2 Nov 2026. Option C needs agreement with the commissioning team before the shutdown roster is published.
Under the hood

How Paramap turns a project plan into this page

Paramap reads the plan you already maintain, an Excel sheet or a Microsoft Project, Primavera P6 or Smartsheet export. Every weekly upload is saved as a snapshot, so each chart can compare versions.

  1. ImportMap the sheet's columns once. Paramap remembers the mapping for next week's upload.
  2. ValidateFlag missing links, open-ended tasks, dates in the past without actuals, and circular dependencies.
  3. SnapshotStore the upload as a version: baseline, approved revision or weekly progress update.
  4. RescheduleRe-run the critical path with real progress, calendars and resource limits. Report what moved and why.
  5. SimulateMonte Carlo over durations, using each trade's actual-vs-planned history as the spread.
  6. TranslateTurn the slip into cost, cash, resource, contract and revenue impacts, and write the reasoning.

Data model

How each prediction is made

OutputMethod
Delay ripple and critical pathCritical path method (forward and backward pass) on the dependency network, run once with v3 dates and once with actual progress. The difference per task is the slip. Float used and calendar effects are recorded as causes.
Forecast dates and P50/P80Monte Carlo over task durations. Each remaining duration is scaled by the trade's performance factor so far (actual ÷ planned duration), with a triangular spread from that trade's history.
S-curve forecastRemaining work is spread along the rescheduled plan. The band is the P10 to P90 of the simulation.
Cost impactTime-related costs (site overheads, hire, supervision) × weeks of slip. Stand-by and remobilisation from resource assignments. Penalties from contract terms entered once per project.
Resource impactAssignments re-spread on the rescheduled dates and compared with the capacity set for each crew or role.
Reasoning textGenerated from the trace of causes, not written freehand, so every sentence links back to a task, link or calendar entry in the plan.
