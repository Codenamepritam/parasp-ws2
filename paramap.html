The problem

"The switchgear is 3 weeks late."

That sentence is in a thousand status reports every week. It is almost never the real number. On large projects, a small delay meets float, shared crews, calendars and contract dates, and comes out bigger on the other side.

  • Q1When will we really finish?

    Not the latest plan date. A forecast from real progress, with the odds of hitting it.

  • Q2What will it cost us?

    Overheads, plant hire, penalties, contingency, cash flow and lost revenue, in one number.

  • Q3What should we do about it?

    Every recovery option, costed and compared, with a clear recommendation.

How 3 weeks became 5

Paramap traces where the time really went.

From the demo project: the supplier delay was 3 weeks. Float absorbed one. The electrical crew, sent to another site while it waited, needed a week to come back. The pushed-out testing then landed in the two-week holiday shutdown. Handover moved 5 weeks, and cost about $2.4M in extra spend and deferred rent.

Questions

Common questions

We already use Primavera or MS Project. Why add Paramap?

Keep them. They are good at building and maintaining the schedule. Paramap reads their exports and adds what they do not provide: forecasting with odds, the cost and resource impact of each delay, and a costed comparison of recovery options in plain language.

Our plan isn't perfectly clean. Will it still work?

Yes. The first upload runs a plan health check that flags missing dependencies, open-ended tasks and dates without progress. Many teams find that check useful on its own.

What does Paramap need from the plan?

At minimum, task IDs, names, start and finish dates, and predecessors. Crews, costs and calendars make the impact analysis richer. Paramap asks for these once and remembers them.

How is the forecast calculated?

Paramap re-runs the critical path with real progress, then simulates the rest of the project thousands of times, using how each trade has actually performed so far. Every forecast traces back to named tasks, links and calendar entries.

How often does it update?

Every time a new plan or progress update is uploaded. Most teams update weekly.
